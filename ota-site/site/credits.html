Know what’s inside.

This page maps the major upstream projects, Radio Sound integration, embedded runtimes, media, and license references behind the developer trial.

Each repository and binary keeps its own notices. This is a practical map, not a replacement for the notices shipped in the image and source trees.

Offline voice stack

Caramel Voice uses Vosk or streaming Zipformer recognition profiles, sherpa-onnx/Kokoro speech, eSpeak NG, JNA, Kotlin, and ONNX Runtime. The repository retains exact revisions, hashes, and notices in its provenance lock.
